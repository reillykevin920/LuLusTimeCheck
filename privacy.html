Privacy Policy

LuLu’s Time Check · Effective October 19, 2026

LuLu’s Time Check is an educational Apple Watch app made to help children practice reading analog clocks and follow daily routines.

Information collected

We do not collect personal information, analytics, or usage data through the app. The app contains no advertising, tracking SDKs, third-party analytics, or account registration and does not connect to our servers.

Information stored on the Watch

Learning progress, schedules, and preferences are stored locally on the Apple Watch. A parent PIN is held locally in Apple’s Keychain. This information is not transmitted to Camp Chair Studios.

Reminders

With permission, LuLu’s Time Check uses local Apple Watch notifications for time practice and configured routines. Families can manage notification permissions through watchOS.

Children’s privacy

The app is designed for children and does not request or collect children’s personal details.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s features or privacy practices change, we will update this page.

Contact

Privacy inquiries can be sent through the Camp Chair Studios contact form.
